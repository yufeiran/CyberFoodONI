[b]Cyber Food | 仿生感官用餐[/b]

Bionic Duplicants do not need food to survive—but they can still enjoy the taste and ritual of dining.
仿生人不靠食物生存，但依然可以享受味道与共同进餐的仪式感。

[i]Bionic Duplicants insist that this is not wasting food, but high-precision sensory calibration.[/i]
[i]仿生人坚持认为，它不是在浪费食物，而是在进行高精度感官校准。[/i]

[h1]Features / 功能[/h1]
[list]
[*]200 kcal per meal → +4 Morale for 3 cycles / 每餐 200 千卡 → +4 士气，持续 3 周期
[*]Optional original food effects and dining-room effects / 可选原版食物效果与餐厅效果
[*]Enabled food and room bonuses also last 3 cycles / 开启后，食物与餐厅加成同样持续 3 周期
[*]Options are disabled by default; configure them from Cyber Food's [b]Options[/b] button / 两项默认关闭，可在模组的“选项”中开启
[*]Effects do not stack; Standard Duplicants are unchanged / 效果不叠加，不改变普通复制人
[/list]
