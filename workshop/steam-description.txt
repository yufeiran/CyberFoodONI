Bionic Duplicants do not need ordinary food for survival—but they can still miss taste, aroma, texture, and the social ritual of dining.

[i]Bionic Duplicants insist that this is not wasting food, but high-precision sensory calibration.[/i]

[b]Core features[/b]
[list]
[*]Allows Bionic Duplicants to taste permitted ordinary food
[*]Consumes only 200 kcal per dining experience
[*]Grants +4 Morale for 3 cycles
[*]The effect does not stack; they eat again only after it expires
[*]Does not change Standard Duplicant behavior
[/list]

[b]Requirement[/b]
The Bionic Booster Pack is required.

[b]Inspiration[/b]
Inspired by the treatment of cyborg food and remembered taste in Ghost in the Shell: S.A.C. 2nd GIG, especially episode 8, “Vegetarian Dinner / FAKE FOOD.” This is an unofficial fan-made mod and contains no Ghost in the Shell assets.

[b]中文说明[/b]

仿生人不需要普通食物维持生存，但它们依然会怀念味道、气味、口感，以及和同伴一起吃饭的仪式感。

[i]仿生人坚持认为，它不是在浪费食物，而是在进行高精度感官校准。[/i]

[list]
[*]可在“饮食”界面允许仿生人食用普通食物
[*]每次仅消耗 200 千卡
[*]获得 +4 士气，持续 3 周期
[*]效果不叠加；结束后才会再次进食
[*]不改变普通复制人的进食行为
[/list]

[b]需要安装仿生人强化包（Bionic Booster Pack）。[/b]
